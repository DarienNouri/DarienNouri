**Projects**
- [Kalshi vs. NOAA](https://github.com/DarienNouri/kalshi-forecast-value): NYC daily-high temperature markets had lower Brier loss than NOAA's forecast at 12 and 6 hours out, over 90 held-out days each. A fitted blend put all its weight on the market.
- [Bayesian lag selection](https://github.com/DarienNouri/bayesian-factor-selection): spike-and-slab selection over 54 candidate predictors (six NYC data series at lags of 0 to 8 months) for NYC home prices. 311 calls at a 2-month lag and evictions at 6- and 8-month lags had the highest inclusion probabilities.
- [City data and real estate](https://github.com/DarienNouri/alt-data-real-estate-predictions): NYC building complaints, evictions and 500M+ Citi Bike trips as leading indicators for home prices and REIT ETFs. Scraping, geocoding, MongoDB and Postgres pipeline.
- [Model complexity in pairs trading](https://github.com/DarienNouri/ml-trading-complexity): spread forecasts for S&P pairs with linear, tree and LSTM models on 280 price and news-sentiment features, backtested with 1% transaction costs. NYU team project, three authors.
